## 4. Client requirements (as stated)

1. **Personal website, not a business site** — no booking/commerce flows. A "compressed Wikipedia" about her: who she is, what she does, her achievements.
2. **Very professional and elegant** design — high-end look.
3. **Fully responsive** — both mobile and desktop.
4. **Multi-page structure with clean URLs** — e.g. `drdeeptiverma.world/about`, `/services` — NOT a single long scrolling page.
5. **Every social link functional** — all buttons direct to the correct live pages.
6. **Deployed publicly** — GitHub Pages, later mapped to www.drdeeptiverma.world.
