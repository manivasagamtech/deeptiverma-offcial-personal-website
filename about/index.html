About Dr. Deepti Verma

A nutritionist, life coach and wellness expert with over 30 years of experience — and a career that spans clinical practice, education, coaching and corporate training.

Home›About

Three decades in nutrition & dietetics

Dr. Deepti Verma is a Nutritionist, Life Coach and Wellness Expert with more than 30 years of experience in nutrition and dietetics. She began her career leading the Nutrition Division at VLCC Healthcare Ltd., one of the region's best-known wellness organizations, where she oversaw wellness and education programs across India, South Asia and the Middle East.

Her academic foundation is equally distinguished: she is a Gold Medallist in Food and Nutrition and holds a Doctorate awarded by the Ministry of Health.

An integrated philosophy

What sets Dr. Verma apart is her refusal to treat the body and the mind as separate projects. She is a Certified Life Coach through the Tony Robbins Academy and an Advanced Yoga Trainer accredited by Yoga Alliance International — credentials that let her bring clinical nutrition expertise and mindset coaching together rather than treating them as separate things.

This integrated approach shapes everything she does: a client working on weight management is also supported on stress and self-belief; a professional building leadership skills is also coached on resilience and wellbeing.

Leading MasterMind Body Global

Today, as Director of MasterMind Body Global (MMBG), Dr. Verma works with individuals on nutrition and weight management — including supporting clients managing diabetes, hypertension and other chronic conditions alongside their existing medical care — as well as on life transformation, mindset and money coaching.

She also works with organizations on corporate leadership, communication and team training, and appears as a speaker at national and international corporates and universities.

Beyond the practice

Outside her practice, Dr. Verma has served as a trainer and judge for the Femina Miss India prelims. She also competes as a state-level athlete, having won Gold and Silver medals for Delhi State — proof that she practices the discipline and vitality she teaches.

Through her YouTube channel Magics & Miracles and her Instagram communities, she shares daily inspiration, nutrition guidance and life-transformation sessions with an audience of tens of thousands.
